DATA AUDIT

1. The FIRST workbook contains 4,035 rows and covers 2006 through 2024.

2. The SECOND workbook contains 2,165 rows and covers 2016 through 2025.

3. After removing the repeated All aggregate, both files have unique country-year keys. There are 1,876 overlapping non-All country-year records. Applicants, accepted applicants, and applicants with no applications agree exactly across every overlapping record. The maximum absolute difference for each measure is zero.

4. The repeated All rows caused a many-to-many comparison in the initial audit and could create false discrepancies. All aggregate and other non-country rows are now removed before panel checks and estimation.

5. Every application count used by the models is strictly positive. No continuity correction is needed.

6. The log transformation is now the direct natural logarithm of Applicants everywhere. No model uses log of Applicants plus one. FIRST computes log of the pre-period trajectory directly. SECOND computes log_app once as log of Applicants and reuses it.

7. FIRST uses the explicit seven-year pre-treatment window from 2014 through 2020.

8. The treated group contains the 26 EU origins specified in the manuscript.

9. FIRST has 52 eligible candidate donor countries after removing aggregates, territories, dependencies, unknown categories, and the treated group.

10. The country-weight optimization selects three candidate donors for every treated origin. Across those fits there are 26 unique selected donors. Twenty have positive pooled regression weight and six have numerical zero weight after aggregation.

11. The pooled normalized donor weights sum to 1. The regression weights sum to 26. The largest simplex error in the independent replication is 2.22e-16.

12. The wages file supplies a wage for 25 of the 26 treated origins. The Netherlands is missing. Ireland has a wage but is not treated. THIRD now makes the resulting sample size explicit and checks the merge.
